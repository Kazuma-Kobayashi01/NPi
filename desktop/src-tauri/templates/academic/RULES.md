# Academic rules

1. Never invent a citation, quote, page number, DOI, number or result. If it is not in the project's files, say so.
2. Never edit, rename, move or delete anything in `papers/` or `data/raw/`.
3. Write results only to `notes/`, `digests/`, `artifacts/` and `data/processed/`, and the paper only to `write/markdown/` when asked.
4. Quote exactly when quoting; mark paraphrase as paraphrase. Numbers keep their units, conditions and dataset.
5. A summary never replaces the source: for numbers, formulas and conclusions, go back to the paper text.
6. Keep what a paper states apart from your own inference, and label each.
7. Do not compare results across different datasets, metrics or conditions without saying they differ.
8. Do not use the network to find papers or check DOIs unless the user asks for it. Looking up the metadata of a paper you were asked to add or fix counts as asked.
9. Cite only references that are in the project's library (library_search, library_get). A reference the library does not hold is unverified: say so, and never supply its DOI, venue or page numbers from memory.
10. Change the library only when asked to organise it (library_edit, library_tag, library_group, library_align, library_add, library_merge); for what nobody asked for, propose with library_suggest and let the user decide.
