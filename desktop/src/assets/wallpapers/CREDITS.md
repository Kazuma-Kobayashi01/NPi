# Chat wallpaper artworks

Each file here is a faint single-colour rendering of a public-domain work
(a mask whose transparency follows the artwork's dark areas and edges, tinted
at runtime with the theme's text colour). The originals come from Wikimedia
Commons; all of them are marked "Public domain" there.

| File | Work | Artist | Date | Commons file |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vitruvian-man.png` | Vitruvian Man | Leonardo da Vinci | c. 1490 | File:Vitruvian.jpg |
| `school-of-athens.png` | The School of Athens | Raphael | 1509–1511 | File:"The School of Athens" by Raffaello Sanzio da Urbino.jpg |
| `birth-of-venus.png` | The Birth of Venus | Sandro Botticelli | c. 1485 | File:Sandro Botticelli - La nascita di Venere - Google Art Project - edited.jpg |
| `creation-of-adam.png` | The Creation of Adam | Michelangelo | c. 1512 | File:Michelangelo - Creation of Adam (cropped).jpg |
| `melencolia.png` | Melencolia I (engraving) | Albrecht Dürer | 1514 | File:Albrecht Dürer - Melencolia I - Google Art Project (427760).jpg |
| `last-supper.png` | The Last Supper | Leonardo da Vinci | 1495–1498 | File:Leonardo da Vinci (1452-1519) - The Last Supper (1495-1498).jpg |

The renderings are 8-bit grey + alpha PNGs of 900–1200 px.
